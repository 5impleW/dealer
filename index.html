    Apply to become a dealer

    Why it earns the floor space.

    A ladder, not one price point

    From an entry Class 2 e-bike to a full street-legal motorcycle. The customer who balks at the top of the range still leaves on something.

    Half the range needs no licence

    Class 2 sells to anyone who walks in — no endorsement, no registration, no trip to the DMV.

    Little to build on arrival

    Depending on the model, units land 80–100% assembled. Your techs do a pre-delivery check, not a build.

    Parts, not lead times

    Racks, keys, grips, tires, chargers and complete battery packs ship from California as their own SKUs.

    Warranty published in full

    12 months / 10,000 miles on motorcycle core components; 3 years / 3,000 miles on the e-bikes. Every exclusion is written down.

    Margin that scales

    Tiered wholesale pricing across the whole lineup. The full schedule goes out once you are approved.
